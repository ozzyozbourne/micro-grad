tinygrad, from the inside

An interactive, from-first-principles tour of the entire tinygrad compiler. You'll follow a tensor expression from a Python call all the way down to generated C and its execution, with every intermediate stage shown, explained and animated. Everything here comes from the actual source at commit 6a404871 (tinygrad 0.14.0, Oct 2026): real UOp graphs, real kernels, real outputs.

The whole machine in one picture
Press play to send a packet (a tensor expression) through the pipeline. Click any stage to jump to the chapter that explains it.

How to use this guide

  • Go in order. Chapters build on each other: first the IR and rewrite engine (Part I), then the tensor graph (II), scheduling (III), codegen (IV), runtime (V), and finally everything together (VI).
  • Run the examples. Every chapter has self-contained scripts in examples/. From the guide folder: DEV=CPU python3 examples/04_uop_basics.py. Add DEBUG=2 (kernel timings), DEBUG=4 (generated code) or VIZ=1 to see more.
  • Play with the demos. Graphs are hoverable (tooltips show the full UOp), steppers can be played or stepped, and most demos use data captured from real tinygrad runs.
  • Follow the ⌥ source links. They point to the exact lines at the pinned commit.
  • Finish with Chapter 22. The live explorer traces any program you type through every stage.
